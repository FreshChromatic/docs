title: Residence
description: Make ChunkRevive avoid chunks protected by Residence claims during selection, regeneration, and deletion.
---

## Where claims are checked

ChunkRevive checks claims during manual and batch marking, region and biome scans, Regen/Reset planning, and immediately before Delete/Prune submits region I/O.

When a new claim, expansion, or subzone takes effect, the plugin removes outstanding marks or queued targets inside it.

## Scan policy

```yaml
scan:
  check-residence-claims: true
```

Turning this off affects only whether scans apply claim policy. It does not disable the final Delete/Prune safety check.

## Partial structure overlap

```yaml
structure:
  residence:
    on-partial-claim: EXCLUDE_CLAIMED
```

| Value | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| `EXCLUDE_CLAIMED` | Exclude the claimed part. |
| `ABORT_WHOLE` | Abort the entire structure job on any overlap. |
| `IGNORE_CLAIMS` | Ignore the partial-overlap policy. |
