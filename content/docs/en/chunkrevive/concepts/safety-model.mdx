---
title: World, claim, and operation safety
description: Restrictions and protection checks used before ChunkRevive creates and runs jobs.
---

Safety is layered rather than a single confirmation button.

## World access policy

`worlds.mode` and `worlds.list` choose eligible worlds. `worlds.scope` separately controls manual marking, bulk marking, structure detection, structure refresh, and regeneration.

```yaml
worlds:
  mode: WHITELIST
  list:
    - resource_world
```

## Confirmation and claims

Bulk regeneration, Reset, Delete, Prune, and large clearing operations show a plan first and require `--confirm` within `safety.confirm-timeout-seconds`. Confirmation belongs to the current plan; do not save old confirmation commands in routine scripts.

With Residence installed, claimed targets are excluded. A deletion job checks again immediately before region I/O because claims can change while it waits.

## Structures, players, and holders

Protected structure groups prevent a complete region from being pruned. Delete/Prune also expand checks by the player-safety padding, require no nearby player or Moonrise ChunkHolder, observe two consecutive cold checks, then recheck holders and Residence before writing.

## Backups are still required

These checks reduce the chance of removing in-use data; they do not provide recovery. A wrong world, coordinate, mark set, or generation environment still requires a backup to restore.
