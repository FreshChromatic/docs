---
title: Monitor, cancel, and recover jobs
description: Inspect regeneration, scan, and deletion status and understand waiting reasons.
---

## Status

```text
/cr status [page]
```

Status pages show batch regeneration, world scans, deletion and region cleanup, and current concurrency, completion, and pending counts.

## Cancel

```text
/cr cancel
```

This stops cancellable regeneration, scans, or waiting deletion jobs. Work already written to world or region storage is not automatically reversed.

## Waiting to become cold

Waiting deletion jobs check for nearby players, spawn chunks or other plugins holding the chunk, the two cold-check observations, and new Residence claims. Do not repeatedly submit the same command; that does not release holders faster.

## After a restart

`deletion.resume-on-startup` defaults to `true`. Unfinished deletion jobs are restored from the database and cold-checked again. Use `/cr status` after restart rather than assuming a job was cancelled or completed.
