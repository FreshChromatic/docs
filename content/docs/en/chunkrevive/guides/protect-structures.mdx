---
title: Detect, protect, and refresh structures
description: Use structure groups to avoid cutting through or overwriting important structures during batch maintenance.
---

ChunkRevive records each detected structure range as a group. Players can inspect groups; administrators can control their refresh and protection state.

## Inspect nearby structures

```text
/cr struct check
/cr struct list [page]
```

`/keep`, `/keepchunk`, and `/chunkkeep` also show nearby passive-protection progress. They do not create a permanent protection entry or modify marks.

## Manage a group

```text
/cr struct block <groupId>
/cr struct unblock <groupId>
/cr struct reset <groupId>
/cr struct regen <groupId>
```

- `block` locks the group manually.
- `unblock` removes its protection state.
- `reset` clears protection state and its refresh timer.
- `regen` forces a refresh of the group.

A forced refresh can be rejected if a third-party claim overlaps the structure range.

## Clear tracked state

```text
/cr struct resetall
/cr struct resetall --confirm
```

This resets every structure group and timer; it is not a routine first troubleshooting step.
