---
title: Delete chunks and prune regions
description: Remove scattered chunks or complete Anvil regions and let the server regenerate them later.
---

Delete/Prune wait for targets to unload and remain cold, then change terrain, entity, and POI storage. They do not generate new terrain immediately.

## Delete: scattered or incomplete targets

```text
/cr delete chunk here
/cr delete chunk <world> <cx> <cz>
/cr delete marked [world]
```

At the player's current position, you can also use:

```text
/cr delete here
/cr delete here chunk
/cr delete here struct
/cr delete here biome
```

Structure, biome, and large marked jobs show a plan and require confirmation. Delete frees reusable `.mca` sectors.

## Prune: complete Anvil regions

```text
/cr prune region here
/cr prune region <world> <rx> <rz>
/cr prune region chunk <world> <cx> <cz>
/cr prune region all
```

`region chunk` chooses the region containing the chunk; direct region selection uses `rx` and `rz`. A region can be truncated only when all 1,024 positions are removable and no structure is protected.

## Prune already empty regions

```text
/cr prune empty <world>
```

This scans index-empty region files and shrinks them after confirmation.
