---
title: Select and mark chunks
description: Build and inspect a target set for regeneration, reset, or deletion.
---

Marking only adds chunks to ChunkRevive's target set; it does not change terrain immediately.

## One chunk

```text
/cr mark here
/cr mark chunk <world> <cx> <cz>
```

`cx` and `cz` are chunk coordinates. Use `here` when you are unsure of coordinates.

## Mark while moving

```text
/cr mark follow
/cr unmark follow
```

Run either command again to toggle its mode.

## Radius and world scans

```text
/cr mark radius <world> <radius> [x] [z]
/cr mark fullmark <world|all>
```

The radius centre `x` and `z` are block coordinates. Large scans can require `--confirm` and, by default, do not run concurrently with Regen.
